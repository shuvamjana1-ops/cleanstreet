🟢

Wet Waste

गीला कचरा / Green Bin

  • 🥕 Vegetable and fruit peels
  • 🍚 Leftover cooked food, rice, sabzi
  • 🌿 Garden leaves, grass clippings
  • ☕ Tea leaves, coffee grounds
  • 🥚 Egg shells
  • 🌸 Flowers used in puja
  • 🐾 Pet waste (bagged separately)
💡 Tip: Keep in a separate container with a lid. Can be composted at home.
🔵

Dry Waste

सूखा कचरा / Blue Bin

  • 📰 Newspapers, magazines, cardboard
  • 🍾 Glass bottles and jars (empty)
  • 🥤 Plastic bottles, containers (rinsed)
  • 🛍 Plastic bags and packaging
  • 🥫 Tin cans, aluminium foil
  • 👕 Old clothes and textiles (clean)
  • 📦 Tetra packs (rinsed, flattened)
💡 Tip: Rinse containers before discarding. Dry waste can be sold to the raddi-wala (scrap dealer).
🔴

E-Waste

इलेक्ट्रॉनिक कचरा / Red Bin

  • 📱 Old mobile phones and chargers
  • 💻 Laptops, tablets, hard drives
  • 🔋 Batteries (AA, AAA, li-ion)
  • 💡 CFL and LED bulbs
  • 🖨 Printers, ink cartridges
  • 📺 Old TVs, remote controls
  • 🔌 Cables and extension cords
💡 Tip: Never mix with regular bins. Find authorised e-waste collection centres via your city's municipal website.
⚫

Hazardous & Sanitary

हानिकारक कचरा / Black Bin

  • 🧴 Medicines and expired drugs
  • 🎨 Leftover paint, solvents
  • 🩺 Used bandages, syringes (needle caps on)
  • 🧹 Used napkins, diapers, sanitary pads
  • 🐀 Pesticide containers (triple-rinsed)
  • 🧪 Cleaning chemicals and bleach bottles
💡 Tip: Always double-bag sanitary waste. Medicines should be returned to a pharmacy if possible.
